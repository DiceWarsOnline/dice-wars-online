      BATTLE REPLAY

      How to play

      SETUP & TURN

      Each deck splits into a unit pile and an equipment pile. Draw 3 from each. At turn start, refill each side of your hand to 3, then roll 2d6.

      PLAYING CARDS

      Each die can play one card whose value is equal to or lower than that die. You may reroll either or both dice once, but not after playing a card. If a valid play exists after the final roll, you must make at least one.

      THE BATTLEFIELD

      Keep up to 3 Heroes or Followers in play. Power and Shield cards require a unit and each unit can hold one of each.

      ATTACKING

      Each unit attacks once per turn. Declaring your first attack ends card plays. Heroes automatically defeat Followers when attacking first. Hero vs Hero: higher wins; a tie destroys both. Followers defeat lower-value units; a tie destroys both.

      POWER & SHIELD

      Power adds its value to an initiating attack. A Shield absorbs an incoming attack when the attack value meets or beats the Shield value. Equipment is discarded with its unit.

      DOUBLES

      Use your deck power once that turn before normal plays, or decline it. A new double after a reroll can trigger the power.

      REROLL

      Before playing a card, reroll either one die or both together, once per turn. A new double can trigger your deck power.

      WINNING & TRUCES

      After your roll, you are eliminated if you have no units in play, no playable unit in hand, and no units left in your draw pile. The last player standing wins. When your unit pile reaches 0, you can offer a truce. Against a CPU, you both roll: win the roll and the truce is accepted; lose and the battle continues. Against another player, they decide whether to accept or decline. An accepted truce ends the battle in a draw.
